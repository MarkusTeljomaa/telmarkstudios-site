Scope and related documents

This appendix accompanies the worldwide Terms and Conditions, including the software licence in section 18. The age, access and advertising sections apply to the US version of Dairy World. The privacy rights section applies to US residents where the relevant law grants those rights.

The privacy sections supplement the general Privacy Notice; they do not replace its data inventory, publisher details, retention schedule or provider disclosures.

Age and access

Dairy World is intended for players aged 16 and over. Before starting the US version, we ask for an age group (under 16, 16–17, or 18 and over), not a date of birth. The declaration and any restrictive access decision are stored on the device. A parent cannot approve access for an under-16 player. If you think the record is wrong, contact contact@telmarkstudios.com. Do not send identity documents, passwords or sign-in codes unless specifically requested through an appropriate verification process.

On supported Google Play installations, the game also checks Play age and approval signals before gameplay and after returning from the background. Play may ask for age sharing or require verification through Google Play. A required verification or pending/declined approval prevents access until resolved. A connection error does not remove an earlier restriction. This check does not verify that an adult has parental responsibility.

Play age signals are used for age-appropriate access. They are not provided to advertising or analytics services, and they do not grant advertising, analytics or purchase consent. The US version does not retain or transmit Google's installation identifier. Ordinary account and purchase controls continue to apply independently of the local declaration.

Advertising privacy choices

Eligible adults may receive personalized rewarded ads where their privacy choices and applicable requirements allow it. Otherwise ads are non-personalized or restricted as required. This is not a claim that an ad request contains no personal data. A US installation does not itself impose blanket restricted data processing or override a privacy choice. Google's privacy messages and privacy choices apply where required. When Google requires a privacy-options entry, it is available in Settings under Ad privacy choices after consent information has been obtained. Withdrawing optional analytics remains a separate setting and does not prevent gameplay.

Requests from US residents

Contact contact@telmarkstudios.com to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information, to exercise an applicable privacy right, or to ask us to reconsider a response. Explain the request and provide an account reference if needed, but never a password or sign-in code. The existing public account-deletion page remains accessible without gameplay permission. We verify requests proportionately and respond within the period required by applicable law. Where an authorized agent may act for you, we may ask for evidence of that authority. We do not penalize you for exercising a protected privacy right.

The rights available to you depend on applicable law. They may include access, correction, deletion, portability, an appeal, or an opt-out of certain uses or disclosures. A US expansion does not remove rights available under the GDPR.
