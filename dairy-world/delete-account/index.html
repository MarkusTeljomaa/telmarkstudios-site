Delete your Dairy World account

Effective 18 October 2026. This notice covers the current prelaunch service. Live advertising and real-money checkout remain disabled.

Request deletion of your account and associated data from Dairy World, provided by Markus Teljomaa (private individual), using the Telmark Studios brand.

Email a deletion request

No email app? Send an email to contact@telmarkstudios.com with subject Dairy World account deletion. No app reinstall, new account or purchase is required.

What to include

Your adult account email or public player ID and whether you are the player or their parent/guardian. We verify ownership or authority before erasure; a public player ID alone is not proof. Never send passwords, sign-in codes or payment-card details.

What is deleted

Account erasure covers the online account and sessions, public profile/player ID, rankings, associated gameplay, inventory/reward records, participant match history, linked analytics, chat age/safety choices, associated messages and moderation records, and prelaunch test receipts, subject to lawful exceptions and other players' rights. Local campaign saves remain on your own device; remove app data separately if desired.

When

We respond to email rights requests without undue delay, within one calendar month of receipt and normally within 30 days. Where a calendar month is shorter, the earlier deadline applies. A legally permitted extension and its reasons are communicated within the original month. Support may verify ownership proportionately, without collecting unnecessary information.

Important limits

Uninstalling or signing out is not account deletion. Refunds and store-managed subscriptions are separate. Some information may need retention for an applicable legal obligation, a proportionate security investigation or other people's rights; we explain relevant exceptions and criteria in our response. Live-database erasure does not mean every provider log or backup is immediately purged. Legacy analytics and provider copies require separate operational review.

In the game

Settings → Delete account & data → acknowledge permanent deletion → Delete my account. Failed requests are not reported as successful. Email is also available if the game or backend is unavailable.
