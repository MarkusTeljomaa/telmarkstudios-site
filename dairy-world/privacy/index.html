Privacy policy

Effective 9 November 2026.

About this policy

This policy explains personal-data handling for Dairy World and the Telmark Studios developer website. It describes the planned Android public release in Finland and the European Economic Area and identifies practices that apply only to other builds. The planned public build has ads, paid checkout and optional game analytics turned off. Some test builds offer additional features. The first open test is intended for players across the EEA who can use the English game and this English notice.

The short version for players

Dairy World saves game information on your device and uses an internet connection for online gameplay. Other players can see your game nickname, avatar and results in online features. Public rankings use a game-generated alias. You can ask us to hide your rankings and keep your account. The game gives you a nickname automatically, and you can choose a different one where allowed. Never put your real name, school, address or contact details in a player name or message. Optional usage analytics is off in the planned public build. You or your parent can contact us to see, correct or delete your information. Both Herd and friends chat are for eligible adults on the online game service. A parent PIN cannot turn on chat for a minor or someone whose age is unknown. Friends, Herds and heart gifts can still be used. If someone makes you uncomfortable, block or report them and tell a trusted adult.

Who is responsible

Markus Teljomaa is the controller for Dairy World and the Telmark Studios website and publishes independently in Finland under the Telmark Studios brand. Telmark Studios is a project brand, not a statement that a company has been formed. Contact contact@telmarkstudios.com for privacy questions, rights requests and help for parents or guardians.

Game accounts and online information

When online services are active, Supabase handles an internal account ID, authentication sessions and connection records. Your online profile includes a separate public player ID, an automatically generated or player-selected nickname, and your chosen avatar. Profiles, ranked results and match results can be visible to other players. Public standings show a game-generated alias and in-game cosmetics instead of custom profile text. Contact privacy support to hide your public ranking entries without deleting your account, game progress or private match history. Depending on the enabled game mode and build, servers store progress, balances, inventory, reward records, attempts, moves, timestamps and match results to provide online play and check fair rewards. Anonymous sign-in is pseudonymous and does not mean that no personal data is processed.

In-game bug reports

When you choose Send report, the game sends your description of the problem, when it happened and where, and any optional game information or manually selected screenshot to a private support inbox in Supabase. You do not need a personal email account. The report is linked internally to your game account to prevent abuse and support account deletion; the private support inbox automatically includes your player ID, derived from that account rather than typed by you. Other players cannot read it. Optional game information contains the game version/build, platform and level, not your email, passwords or full game save. Include hardware information is a separate permission that starts off for each new report form. Only after you choose it, the game reads available device model/manufacturer, OS version, RAM, CPU architecture/core count and screen size/density for troubleshooting. You can review the frozen details or remove them before sending; the game does not read serial numbers, advertising IDs, Android ID, contacts, location or installed-app lists for this feature. This permission is not saved across report forms, is not analytics consent, and declining it does not prevent reporting or affect rewards. Submitted hardware details follow the same private support purpose, account linkage and retention as the report; contact us to request erasure of details already submitted. The selected screenshot is resized and re-encoded without its original filename or image metadata. Do not include real names, contact details, passwords, sign-in codes or other private information. Reports are used to investigate game problems. A reference confirms receipt; failures leave the draft in the open form for retry. Unsent drafts are not saved after closing the form. Reports and their screenshots become eligible for deletion after 30 days and are removed in bounded batches by the existing hourly cleanup, or when the associated game account is deleted. This does not erase separate provider logs/backups or private operator exports immediately. The separate Contact developer option still opens your email app; correspondence you choose to send there follows the support-email handling below.

Local diagnostic log

The game keeps a bounded log of up to 128 recent technical events in local process memory to help investigate faults. It records fixed operation and outcome codes, relative timings and limited numeric counters, not names, account IDs, messages, email addresses, URLs, tokens, passwords, exception text or stack traces. It is not written to a game log file and has no automatic upload or analytics connection. It disappears when the process ends and is cleared on explicit account logout. Developer settings offers a local viewer, clear and copy controls. When sending a bug report, Include recent diagnostics is initially off. If you choose it, you can review a short frozen snapshot before sending; that snapshot then follows the same private support access, account linkage and deletion/retention rules as the report. Copies you explicitly place on your clipboard or share separately are outside the game's local log buffer.

Google Play Games platform connection

Android builds integrate Google Play Games Services for platform authentication when online features are allowed and the Play Games project is configured. Google processes the connected Play Games profile, app/device and connection information. Automatic profile-creation prompts are suppressed; Settings offers explicit connection. Connection failure does not block gameplay. If you separately enable Google backup & sync, the game obtains a one-use authorization code and sends it securely to our backend to verify the game-scoped Google player ID. The backend keeps a private link between that ID and your original Dairy World account so you can recover the same account on another phone. The game does not read your email, contacts, friend list, Google profile name or picture. No Google access or refresh token is kept by our backend. Server-confirmed achievements and campaign scores or peak PvP ratings can be shared with Google Play Games; their visibility follows your Google profile choices. Saved Games stores a separate backup of career history, replay puzzles, visual selections and ordinary game preferences in Google's app data storage. It excludes balances, owned inventory, ad claims, authentication sessions, family/privacy choices and developer settings. Purchases and rewards remain authoritative on the Dairy World server; restoration does not merge accounts or grant additional permissions. You can pause sync or remove this game variant's Google backup in Settings. Pausing or deleting your Dairy World account stops new app-initiated Play Games calls and account erasure removes the private backend link, but does not delete your Google account or Play Games profile. Manage Google-held achievements, leaderboard records and remaining cloud copies separately in Play Games/Google settings. A remaining backup cannot recover a deleted Dairy World account.

Your choices and local information

An initial account connection downloads campaign puzzles and reserves an inventory allowance. Downloaded PvE puzzles can then be played offline. Puzzle data, move logs, local ratings and pending results remain on your device and are uploaded for server validation after reconnection. Offline timing is not trusted for competitive rankings. PvP, purchases and ad verification require internet. Saved progress, preferences and cached art can remain on your device. Usage analytics is a separate optional choice, initially off. Turning it off removes unsent optional events and the local analytics ID, but does not itself erase earlier uploads or essential online-service and security records. When you try to open adult-only Herd or friends chat, the game asks for an age group: under 18, 18 or older, or prefer not to say. It saves this group on the server, not your birthday. This is a self-declaration, not verified age or parental consent. No parent-email permission flow is used. A pause recorded for account deletion survives restart and requires a deliberate choice before reconnecting. Removing app data clears local information; uninstalling alone does not delete a server account.

Community features

Where community features are enabled, they can store friend requests, blocking choices, herd membership and invitations, contributions, reward records, messages, read markers and reports. Both Herd and friends chat require adult eligibility on the online game service. Accounts recorded as under 18 or with unknown age cannot read or send chat messages. Both people in a direct conversation must be accepted friends and meet the age and chat requirements. Adults must acknowledge a safety reminder. Family controls can permit non-chat social features for minors. Older app versions may show a separate friends-chat switch, but it cannot grant access to typed chat on the game service. Friends, Herd membership and heart gifts do not require chat permission. Adults can turn chat off in Friends & Herds. A recorded under-18 age group cannot be changed to adult through the app; contact the privacy address for a genuine correction. Recipients or eligible channel members can see messages. Blocking, reporting, text filters and operator message removal or temporary chat restrictions support safety, but cannot detect all harmful content. Chat is not monitored live. Moderation records include a reason category and time; authorized operators can review reported messages. Child-safety, age-assurance and moderation operations require review before public release.

Email sign-in and purchases

The planned public Android build has paid checkout and purchase-account sign-in disabled. The following applies where purchase accounts or checkout are enabled. Purchase-account sign-in uses an email address and sign-in code, or an explicit Google sign-in. Supabase verifies the chosen account; Resend delivers sign-in email, including delivery and security records. Google sign-in sends a Google identity token to Supabase to authenticate the account; it is separate from Play Games. Do not enter a child's email in an adult purchase-account flow. Google Play handles Android payments and purchase approval, including any applicable family approval. Dairy World does not collect payment-card numbers. Our server verifies the purchase token, store transaction, product, time, status and bound game account before delivering items. Google license-test purchases do not charge the test card and are marked separately from paid purchases. Test-card access and any server test-purchase admission are operator-controlled.

Optional usage analytics and fair-play checks

The planned public Android build does not send optional usage analytics. If separately enabled, first-party optional analytics records game, shop, reward, ad and purchase-flow events with random installation, session and event IDs, build/platform information, durations and allowlisted campaign tags. Its event properties exclude free-text names, email addresses, card details, raw receipt tokens, advertising IDs and raw motion readings. Account-linked security, reward and fair-play records may still be required to provide an online feature even if optional analytics is off. Those records are not permission to track you for advertising. Legacy analytics without a reliable account link requires operator review when handling erasure requests.

Advertising and Google

The planned public Android build does not request ads. Dairy World integrates Google AdMob and the User Messaging Platform (UMP). Android previews and invited-adult private test releases may request Google test ads; live ads remain disabled. Eligible Android test builds prepare UMP and the ads SDK after startup, before you choose a rewarded placement. SDK preparation may contact Google; choosing not to watch an ad is not a promise that no SDK request occurred. Ad requests use G-rated, child-directed, under-age-of-consent and non-personalized settings for everyone. The Android manifest removes advertising-ID and AdServices identifier permissions. These settings reduce processing but do not mean that ad requests send no data. When an ad service is used, Google can receive IP address (which can indicate general location), device/app information, ad views and interactions, diagnostic information and identifiers permitted by the platform and request settings. Google uses advertising-service information to deliver ads, measure performance, maintain security and prevent fraud. We do not request precise device location for ads. Google's information pages linked below explain its processing.

Reward verification and advertising choices

Rewarded ads are voluntary. Watching an ad is not consent to optional game analytics. Where server-verified rewards are enabled, a pseudonymous account ID and reward-claim reference may be sent to Google, and our server receives the signed ad/reward transaction information to validate a one-time grant and prevent duplicate rewards. UMP checks current advertising eligibility before ad preparation. For eligible users, a required Google privacy message offers Consent, Do not consent and Manage options; required Ad privacy choices can be reopened from Settings. The current under-age treatment prevents UMP from requesting consent, so publishing a console message does not make it appear for those requests. We do not treat a child's ad interaction, a store approval or a puzzle answer as parental consent. Required child protections must be reviewed before any live advertising release.

Motion, downloads and website visitors

Motion readings used for shake controls stay on the device and are not included in the game analytics event schema. Art downloads and online requests expose IP address and request metadata to their service providers. The developer website and these legal pages use no publisher-added advertising, analytics scripts, tracking cookies or contact forms. They are hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs visitor IP addresses for security, including visitors who are not signed in to GitHub. Emailing us opens your own email service and sends the information you choose to provide.

Purposes and legal bases

We process account, gameplay and purchase-verification data to provide the service you request, relying on a contract where that basis is legally applicable. We publish verified competition results under game-generated aliases for our legitimate interest in operating shared standings. You can object and ask us to hide your rankings without losing your game progress. We use proportionate security, abuse-prevention and reliability records for legitimate interests in protecting the service and its players, subject to your applicable rights. We process records required by law to meet legal obligations. Optional analytics and any consent-dependent advertising processing require the applicable separate consent or authorization. You can withdraw consent prospectively; this does not undo processing that was lawful before withdrawal. This notice is not a request to consent to every purpose.

Children and parents

The intended game audience is players aged 13 and above. Players under 13 may not use the game. In builds with startup Terms acceptance enabled, the player must check an initially unchecked declaration that they are at least 13. The dated, versioned acknowledgement is stored on the device. Earlier test builds can show the legal documents without the startup declaration. This is a self-declaration, not independent age or guardianship verification. Ages 13–17 remain minors; a 13+ declaration does not remove applicable parental-consent requirements or enable adult features. An age group or age signal is not parental consent to every use of personal data. Accounts recorded as under 18 or with unknown age cannot use either Herd or friends chat on the online game service. For those accounts, custom public player and Herd names require approval of that exact name in Family controls; otherwise safe aliases are used. Friends and Herd interactions require parent approval and can be switched off with the parent PIN. Ordinary free PvP and the free reward wheel do not require social approval. A parent or guardian sets a private 6–12 digit PIN after an adult birthday check and a responsibility declaration. The birthday is transmitted securely for the check and is not stored in the game database; the PIN is stored only as a salted hash, with approval settings and temporary failed-attempt/lockout records. This is a parent-action safeguard, not independent identity or guardianship verification or a legal-consent receipt. Keep the PIN private. Lost PINs and age corrections go through privacy support. Supported Google Play installations check Play Age Signals when entering social or family controls and recheck after returning to the social screen or when the check is stale. Google may ask you or your parent to share an age range; sharing can be declined or unavailable. The age band and assurance source are used transiently in the app. The game asks for a coarse age group once when it is needed for social or public-name features and remembers that group and the selection time with the game account, including a choice not to disclose. It does not save a birthday for this choice. An unavailable Play check does not replace a saved adult declaration. Known-minor and approval-blocked results still apply and cannot be cleared by selecting adult. When a result requires protection, an account-linked restrictive decision (unknown, minor or approval blocked), an approval-blocked flag and the check time are saved on our server. We do not send the raw age range, assurance source, birthday or Google installation ID to the game server, analytics or advertising. An adult Play response does not automatically grant permissions or clear a previous minor or approval restriction. An explicit adult age-group choice may resolve an unknown-only restriction when no parent controls or recorded minor status apply. Age declarations are not verified adulthood and do not enable personalized advertising, analytics or purchases. Corrections to a recorded minor or approval restriction go through privacy support; a parent PIN may separately manage names and non-chat social features. Older app versions may still show a separate friends-chat control; it cannot grant chat access on the online game service. Age sharing is not parental consent, analytics consent or ad eligibility. Ordinary free gameplay remains available with protected defaults. Parents can contact the privacy address to request access, correction, restriction or deletion. We verify authority proportionately. Where the law requires a parent or guardian to accept the agreement on a player’s behalf, the adult must do so before the player uses the service.

How long information is kept

Local saves remain until you remove them or clear app data. Server account, profile and gameplay records are kept to maintain the account and relevant game history, until deletion or another applicable retention limit. Optional analytics queued on the device expires after 30 days. The game backend runs hourly scheduled cleanup for raw analytics older than 90 days, inactive installation summaries after 90 days and ingestion rate-limit records after two days. Each run uses bounded batches; eligible records are removed at the next successful run, with any backlog processed by later runs. Aggregate cleanup records and this job's execution history are pruned after 30 days. Ordinary chat messages expire after 30 days; reported messages may remain for investigation for up to 90 days from sending. Related reports are removed with the message. Minimal moderation action records expire after 90 days. The same hourly job handles chat in bounded batches. Age-group, chat-safety choices, restrictive age decisions and check times, parent PIN hashes and scoped family approvals remain until account deletion or an appropriate correction. A parent can revoke social/name approvals or change their PIN. Family records are removed when the associated online account is deleted; resetting gameplay does not remove these safety settings. Cleanup does not delete an active account simply because it has been idle. The separate billing test backend runs the same schedule and skips game tables it does not contain. We handle privacy/support correspondence in Google Workspace for the request and necessary follow-up, then remove it when it is no longer needed, subject to a documented legal or security reason for retention. Mailbox cleanup is manual. We review privacy and safety case records 90 days after closure and delete them unless a documented legal, security or ongoing case reason requires us to keep them. Gmail normally keeps deleted mail in Trash for 30 days and allows administrator recovery for another 25 days after permanent deletion; retention rules or legal holds may apply separately. The accepted Workspace data-processing terms allow up to 180 days for provider-system deletion after customer-unrecoverable deletion, subject to legal storage duties. Resend states that its standard active plans keep email and log data for 30 days and backups for seven days. Other provider logs and backups follow provider settings and applicable retention obligations; deletion from the live game database is not a promise of immediate erasure from every provider copy. We assess retained records when fulfilling a request and explain any applicable exception and retention criterion. Operational backup/legacy-data deletion checks remain part of prelaunch release review. The private Play Games recovery link lasts until Dairy World account deletion. Google-held Saved Games, achievements and leaderboard records follow your Google account controls and Google retention; use Remove Google backup before deleting your Dairy World account if you also want the app to remove that variant’s snapshot. For verified real-money purchases, a separate private purchase/dispute record keeps the store and transaction references, product, purchase time, granted rewards, status changes, a one-way account reference and, on erasure, the remaining paid-item summary. It does not copy your email, name, profile, gameplay history, raw purchase token or payment-card details. This record is retained for six complete calendar years after the transaction year, then removed by scheduled cleanup; for example, a 2026 transaction expires on 1 January 2033. It survives account deletion for accounting and proportionate dispute handling. Operational receipts needed to provide or restore purchases remain while the account exists and are erased with the account. Google keeps its own payment and merchant records under its rules. This app record does not replace the publisher's separate accounting obligations. Free Google TEST receipts are excluded from this six-year archive.

Security

Online connections use HTTPS. Server authorization restricts access to account data. Android backup rules exclude stored sign-in sessions; other locally stored game information may be backed up through your device settings. No system can guarantee absolute security. Do not send us passwords, sign-in codes, payment-card details or unnecessary information about a child.

Delete your account and associated data

You can request deletion without reinstalling the app, making a new account or buying anything. Use the deletion page linked below or email contact@telmarkstudios.com with subject Dairy World account deletion. Include your adult account email or public player ID and say whether you are the player or a parent/guardian. A public ID alone is not proof of ownership. We verify ownership or authority before disclosure or erasure. Automatic in-app deletion is enabled on the game and billing test backends. While signed in, choose Settings > Delete account & data, acknowledge permanent server deletion and select Delete my account. The server verifies your current identity and session before deleting associated live records. Use email whenever an in-app request is unavailable or not confirmed. A failed request is not reported as successful. Account erasure covers associated account/profile, game and reward records, linked analytics, age-group/chat choices, associated messages and moderation records, and operational purchase receipts, subject to lawful exceptions and the rights of other players. Local device saves need separate removal. Refunds and store-managed subscriptions are handled separately by the relevant store.

Your rights and complaints

Where applicable, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, withdraw consent, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. You can ask for a portable JSON/CSV copy where the right applies. Contact the privacy email; Settings > Your data rights can help prepare an email, including a request to hide public rankings, but does not send or fulfill it automatically. Requests are normally free. We respond without undue delay and within one calendar month of receipt, normally within 30 days; where a calendar month is shorter, that earlier deadline applies. Any legally permitted extension and its reasons are communicated within the original month. We may need proportionate information to verify identity or parental authority. If we cannot fulfill all of a request, we explain the reason and applicable complaint options. You may complain to Finland's Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman or another competent supervisory authority. Mandatory rights are not limited by this notice.

Changes to this policy

We update this policy when the service, providers or data uses materially change. A new version and effective date will appear here and in the game, with additional notice or new choices where required.
